          Privacy

          • Raw audio is never stored. It is transcribed in memory and dropped.
          • Only you can trigger a lookup. RainB listens for your own hesitation, not other people’s.
          • Other people’s words are deleted after 24 hours. Your own words and the facts extracted from them stay until you delete them.
          • The demo brain remembers nothing you say. Uploaded notes are deleted after 24 hours.
