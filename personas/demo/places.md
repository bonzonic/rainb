# Places

## Food

- The ramen place near the office is Menya Kagura on Jalan Telawi; get the yuzu shio.
- Best nasi lemak for team breakfasts: Village Park in Damansara Uptown.
- The Korean barbecue place we went to for Daniel's welcome lunch is Daorae in Solaris Mont Kiara.
- The coffee place with the good pour-over near Mid Valley is Pickle & Fig.
- The Italian restaurant where Priya took the team after launch is Nerovivo in Bukit Ceylon.

## Everything else

- My dentist is Dr Lee at Smile Studio, Bangsar South.
- The bookshop with the second-hand design books is Lit Books in Petaling Jaya.
- The co-working space with the quiet phone booths is Common Ground in Bukit Bintang.
- The hotel we booked for the Penang trip is the Cheong Fatt Tze Blue Mansion.
